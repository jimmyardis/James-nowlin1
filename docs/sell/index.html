How selling a home works in South Carolina

Selling a home in South Carolina starts with pricing from recent nearby sales and completing the state's property disclosure form. Then you market the home, choose the offer most likely to close, get through the buyer's due diligence period, and close with an attorney who pays off your loan and sends your proceeds.

See what your home is worth

  1. Price it from real sales

    James prepares a comparative market analysis from recent nearby sales and adjusts for your home's condition and updates. Start with the online estimate if you just want a ballpark.

  2. Prepare the home

    Small fixes, a deep clean and clearing clutter usually return more than they cost. James will tell you what's worth doing and what to skip.

  3. Complete your disclosures

    Most SC sellers fill out the Residential Property Condition Disclosure Statement. Being thorough now prevents problems later in the deal.

  4. Market and show it

    Professional photos, an MLS listing that reaches every agent in the region, and showings scheduled around your life.

  5. Choose the strongest offer

    James compares price alongside financing type, due diligence terms, the buyer's lender and closing date, so you know which offer is most likely to close.

  6. Get through due diligence

    Expect an inspection and possibly repair requests. James negotiates on your behalf and keeps the deal on schedule.

  7. Close

    The closing attorney handles the payoff of your mortgage, the deed and the disbursement of your proceeds.

Seller questions

Do sellers have to disclose problems in South Carolina?

Yes. Most sellers must complete the South Carolina Residential Property Condition Disclosure Statement, which covers known issues with the roof, structure, systems and more. Some sales, like certain estate and foreclosure sales, are exempt. Being upfront protects you and helps the deal hold together.

What is my home worth?

The best answer is a comparative market analysis based on recent nearby sales. You can get an instant estimate from James's home value tool, then ask James for a detailed review that accounts for your home's condition and updates.

When is the best time to sell a home in the Columbia area?

Spring usually brings the most buyers in the Midlands, but a well-priced, well-prepared home sells in any season. Fewer competing listings in the fall and winter can work in your favor.

How much does it cost to sell a house in South Carolina?

Sellers typically pay the real estate commission (which is negotiable), the deed recording fee of $3.70 per $1,000 of sale price, their share of the attorney's fees, any loan payoff and prorated property taxes. James gives you a written estimate of your net proceeds before you list.
