New to South Carolina? Do these in order

Each step unlocks the next. The 4% primary-residence tax rate can save you hundreds a year, but the counties won't approve it until your SC license and car registration show your new address.

  1. Pay the county vehicle property tax

    South Carolina collects property tax on cars and trucks, and the DMV won't register your vehicle until it's paid. Contact your county auditor for the bill, then pay the treasurer.

    Richland County AuditorLexington County Auditor

  2. Register your vehicles with the SC DMV

    New residents have 45 days to title and register vehicles. Bring the paid tax receipt, your out-of-state title, insurance details and the odometer reading.

    SC DMV

  3. Get your SC driver's license

    You can drive on your old state's license for up to 90 days. Use your new address, because the next step depends on it.

    SC DMV driver's licenses

  4. Apply for the 4% legal residence rate

    This is the big one. Your primary home is taxed on 4% of its value instead of 6%, but only after you apply. You'll need your SC license and vehicle registration showing the new address. Apply by the first penalty date on your tax notice.

    Richland County applicationLexington County application

  5. Register to vote

    Update your voter registration to your new address.

    SC Votes

Quick answers

How do I find out who provides water and sewer to a home?

Water and sewer providers vary by neighborhood, and many rural homes use a well and septic system. Check the listing, ask the seller's agent, or use Richland County's utility provider list. James confirms the providers for any home before you make an offer.

Do you pay property tax on cars in South Carolina?

Yes. Vehicles are taxed by the county each year, and you must pay the tax before the SC DMV will register or renew a vehicle. New residents have 45 days to register.

When should I apply for the 4% legal residence rate?

As soon as you've closed and have an SC driver's license and vehicle registration showing your new address. Applications are due by the first penalty date on that year's tax notice. Apply online with the Richland or Lexington County assessor.
